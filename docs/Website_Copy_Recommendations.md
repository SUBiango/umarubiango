# Website Copy Recommendations (Founder Voice)

## Goal
Sharpen the site copy so it sounds clearer, bolder, and more distinctive while staying grounded in your current brand.

## 1) Hero
**Current direction:** Generic builder intro  
**Recommended copy:**  
> I build tools for places the internet forgot.

**Optional sub-line:**  
> Practical software for hard markets, built in public.

---

## 2) Notes Intro
**Current direction:** Broad “reflections/writings” framing  
**Recommended copy:**  
> Field notes from building in Sierra Leone—what breaks, what survives, what scales.

---

## 3) Newsletter Terminal Block (`want_more`)
**Current direction:** Soft, general signup copy  
**Recommended copy:**  
> No growth hacks. Just hard-won lessons worth stealing.

**Optional supporting line:**  
> Occasional essays on product, payments, and building where systems are fragile.

---

## 4) Contact Page CTA
**Current direction:** Generic “Get in touch” language  
**Recommended copy:**  
> If you’re building for hard markets, I’ll reply.

**Optional microcopy near form:**  
> Partnerships, product questions, and serious collaboration only.

---

## 5) Lab Section Intro
**Current direction:** Broad experiments framing  
**Recommended copy:**  
> Small bets, shipped fast, measured honestly.

---

## 6) How I Think Opener
**Current direction:** General personal-thinking intro  
**Recommended copy:**  
> I optimize for clarity over cleverness, and outcomes over noise.

---

## Tone Guardrails (So future copy stays consistent)
- Short sentences over long explanations.
- Concrete language over abstract claims.
- Evidence and earned confidence over hype.
- Write like an operator, not a marketer.

Principles

↑ the back of the poda-poda, unpacked

IF MY CODE WERE A PODA-PODA, THE BACK WOULD SAY:

  • Optimize for constraints
  • Clarity over cleverness
  • Systems over features

On building

  • Ship early.

    Learn from real users, not assumptions. The first version exists to be corrected.

  • Complexity is a liability.

    Kill it on sight. Every moving part is something that breaks on a Friday night.

  • Performance over trend.

    A fast, plain thing beats a slow, fashionable one. Always.

On emerging markets

  • 3G is not an edge case.

    It's the baseline. Design for the connection most people actually have.

  • Payment failure is not a bug.

    It's a business problem. Treat retries, reconciliation and refunds as product, not plumbing.

  • Regulatory friction is a moat.

    If you learn to navigate it. Most people won't, and that's the point.

On learning

  • Read primary sources.

    Summaries are lossy. Go to the spec, the paper, the law.

  • Build the thing to understand the thing.

    Reading about a system and running one are different kinds of knowing.

  • Teach it.

    Writing forces precision. That's most of why this notebook exists.

— Umaru
